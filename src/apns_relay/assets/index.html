Your backend→Signed request→Push relay→APNs→Your app
01 / OWNERSHIP

Your infrastructure. Your keys.

Run the relay yourself. Operator-held Apple signing keys stay under your control while tenant backends use signed requests.

02 / PRIVACY

Less data by design.

No device table. No payload retention. Destination tokens are stored as keyed blind indexes, with redacted operational logs.

03 / CONTROL

Guardrails built in.

App Attest enrollment, destination-bound grants, quotas, rate limits, and revocation help keep delivery accountable.
